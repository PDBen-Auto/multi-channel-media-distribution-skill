# 合成案例：七渠道实际改稿示例

全部为虚构概念的教学文案，无真实产品或购买链接。没有运行时规则/账号检查，不可直接当发布就绪稿。读者稿与内部条件分开；每条只说已定义的概念，不写参数/价格/适配/性能。

## Facebook

**主稿（图片序列）**

> A first look at an illuminated outdoor-accessory concept.
>
> This preview focuses on the shape and accent-lighting idea. Design visuals and any bench-sample footage should be considered separately; neither confirms final specifications or availability.
>
> What would you want to see verified before evaluating a concept like this?

**短版**

> Concept preview: shape, detail and accent lighting. Which detail should be verified next?

内部：三图分别承担整体/细节/设计问题，若没有真实三图就不声称序列已完成；核个人/Page/Group和可见性。ALT描述实际图而非宣传语。

## Reddit

**标题**

> What evidence would help you evaluate an illuminated accessory concept?

**正文**

> I'm exploring how to present an outdoor-accessory concept without turning a preview into a performance claim. The available material would show design visuals and a separate bench sample, not confirmed real-world use.
>
> My checklist starts with final specifications, mounting details and a clear source label for every visual. What would you add before considering the idea seriously?

**替代：只问方法**

> When a product preview includes renders and a different bench sample, what disclosure makes the distinction useful rather than confusing?

内部：这是示例叙事，实际身份必须重写；没有当前社区许可、真实关系说明和flair就不投递。不能用假体验/假普通用户包装推广。

## LinkedIn

**主稿**

> Product previews create a useful design problem: how do we show an idea without suggesting that every detail is already validated?
>
> For this illuminated outdoor-accessory concept, I would keep design visualization separate from bench-sample evidence, state the open specifications and ask which verification matters most to the intended user.
>
> A clear preview should help the next decision, not hide the remaining work. What would you put on that verification checklist?

**短版**

> A product preview is a communication contract: label the visual, state what it proves and make the next verification question useful. What is most often missing from that contract?

内部：根据真实发布者身份改写，不冒充设计者、员工或品牌官方；核个人/公司页、利益关系与实际媒体。没有图就不称图文编辑完成。
